# Charts

BTC/ZAR and ETH/ZAR candle charts (VALR data, Binance fallback) drawn with TradingView Lightweight Charts, made to live on an iPhone home screen. Your key levels and open trade show as lines.

- Open https://waldo881.github.io/Charts/ in Safari, tap Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
- Toggle BTC/ETH and 1D/4h/1h. "Levels & trade" adds your weekly-prep levels and entry/stop/target, saved on the phone.
- **Trading Desk** opens the journal, weekly prep and coach. **Luno** opens the exchange.

Prices come from VALR (close to Luno, not identical). Always check Luno before placing an order.
